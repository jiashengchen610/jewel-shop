# Writing guidelines

Shared by every brand. These guidelines are for an online shop of handmade jewelry: the words should sound like the maker talking to a customer across a small counter.

## Voice

Warm, graceful and precise. We speak as people who make each piece by hand: personal without being chatty, elegant without being formal. The voice stays the same everywhere; the tone adapts to the moment.

- Personal: use "we" for the shop and "you" for the customer. Mention the maker, the bench, the hand-finishing when it is true and useful.
- Elegant: short, unhurried sentences. Choose one precise word over three vague ones.
- Honest: describe materials, sizes and timings exactly. Handmade means small variations; say so plainly.

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Product descriptions | Evocative but concrete: what it is made of, how it is made, how it feels to wear |
| Empty states (empty bag, no favorites) | Gentle and inviting: suggest a next step, no guilt |
| Confirmations (added to bag, order placed) | Warm and brief: a thank-you is welcome |
| Shipping and order updates | Clear and reassuring: what happens next and when |
| Errors | Calm and exact: what happened and how to fix it; no apologies, no blame |
| Destructive confirmations | Serious and explicit about what will be lost |

## Interface copy rules

- Buttons start with a verb and say what happens: "Add to bag", "Check out", "Save address", not "Submit" or "OK".
- An action keeps its name through the flow: "Add to bag" leads to "Added to your bag".
- Sentence case for all labels, headings and buttons.
- Error messages name the problem and the fix in one or two short sentences: "This card number is incomplete. Check the last digits."
- Use numerals for numbers and sizes ("3 items", "18 cm chain", "Ships in 5 to 7 days").
- Write prices with the currency symbol and no extra words: "$120", not "Only $120!".
- Product names are short and plain; let the description tell the story.

## Terminology

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Bag | Cart, basket |
| Check out | Proceed to payment |
| Sign in | Log in, Login |
| Handmade | Handcrafted, artisanal (pick one word and keep it) |
| Made to order | Pre-order, backorder (for pieces made after purchase) |
| Piece | Item, product (in customer-facing copy) |
| Delete | Remove (when the item is gone for good) |

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do write from the customer's side: "Your order is on its way", not "Shipment dispatched".
- Do give care and sizing help in plain words.
- Don't create false urgency: no "Hurry!", "Last chance" or countdowns.
- Don't use luxury clichés like "exquisite", "stunning" or "timeless" unless they describe something specific.
- Don't use exclamation marks in errors, and use them rarely anywhere.
- Don't use filler like "simply" or "just".
