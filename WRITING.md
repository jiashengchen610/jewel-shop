# Writing guidelines

Shared by every brand. These guidelines are for a budgeting app for young professionals: the words should sound like a friend who is good with money, not a bank and not a lecture.

## Voice

Friendly, clear and on your side. The voice stays the same everywhere; the tone adapts to the moment.

- Friendly: talk to "you" in plain, everyday words. Contractions are fine ("you're", "let's").
- Not preachy: describe what happened, never judge it. No "should", no guilt, no lectures about saving.
- Clear: lead with the number or the fact, then the detail. Short sentences that fit a phone screen.
- Honest: exact amounts and dates; no vague "soon" or "a bit".

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Onboarding, empty states | Encouraging and direct: one next step, no pressure |
| Over budget, low balance | Neutral and helpful: the facts and an option, never blame |
| Goals reached, months under budget | Warm and brief: a small celebration is welcome |
| Reminders and notifications | Light and useful: why it matters now, easy to dismiss |
| Errors | Calm and exact: what happened and how to fix it; no apologies, no blame |
| Destructive confirmations | Serious and explicit about what will be lost |

## Interface copy rules

- Buttons start with a verb and say what happens: "Add expense", "Save budget", "Connect account", not "Submit" or "OK".
- An action keeps its name through the flow: "Add expense" leads to "Expense added".
- Sentence case for all labels, headings and buttons.
- Keep labels short enough for a phone: 1 to 3 words for buttons and tabs.
- Error messages name the problem and the fix in one or two short sentences: "That amount is missing a number. Enter how much you spent."
- Use numerals and currency symbols: "$45 left", "3 bills due this week".
- Say overspending plainly: "$20 over your food budget", not "You overspent!" or "Careful!".
- Offer options, not orders: "Want to move $20 from Fun?" instead of "You should cut back".

## Terminology

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Left to spend | Remaining balance, available funds |
| Spending | Expenditure, outgoings |
| Budget | Spending limit, allowance |
| Goal | Target, savings objective |
| Over budget | Overspent, in the red |
| Sign in | Log in, Login |
| Delete | Remove (when the item is gone for good) |

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do write from the user's side: "Your rent is due Friday", not "Scheduled payment pending".
- Do explain money terms in plain words the first time they appear.
- Don't moralize about purchases ("Another coffee?") or compare users to others.
- Don't use finance jargon or bank-speak.
- Don't use exclamation marks in errors or money warnings; use them rarely anywhere.
- Don't use filler like "simply" or "just".
